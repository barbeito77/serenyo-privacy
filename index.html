Serenyo Privacy Policy Version 1.1 — 2 November 2026 1. Who we are Serenyo is operated by Ruben Barbeito, trading as Serenyo. For privacy questions, contact us at serenyo.app@gmail.com. 2. What this policy covers This Privacy Policy explains how Serenyo handles information when you use the Android app, website, and related services, including the optional AI Insights feature. Serenyo is designed to work without an account, with personal reflections stored locally by default. 3. Our privacy principles Local-first by default: Reflections remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to send selected reflections for AI analysis. Data minimisation: We collect only information needed to provide and protect the services you choose to use. No surprise AI: AI processing requires an explicit action and consent. No medical profiling: Serenyo supports personal reflection and wellbeing. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. 4. Information you may enter into Serenyo Depending on how you use Serenyo, you may enter: Mood or emotion selections. Reflection notes or journal entries. Contributing factors, such as sleep, work, family, stress or social situations. Dates, times and connections associated with your reflections. Preferences and settings. Reflections may include sensitive personal or health-related information. 5. Local storage and optional AI processing Serenyo stores your reflections locally on your device. You can use Serenyo without enabling AI Insights. If you choose an AI review, Serenyo sends the selected reflections' full text, dates, emotions, connections and source identifiers over an encrypted connection to Serenyo's gateway and OpenAI. Only the selected reflections are submitted. The review does not happen automatically. Accepted AI insights are stored locally on your device. Serenyo's gateway does not store complete reflection texts or generated insight content in its Firestore database. It retains limited operational records described below. 6. Usage analytics Serenyo does not currently collect behavioural usage analytics or use private reflections for advertising analytics. The AI gateway retains limited operational information for access control, request management, security and spending protection. This is not used for advertising or behavioural profiling. 7. Device and technical information When you use AI Insights, Serenyo uses an installation-specific public key and related identifiers to authenticate requests, verify Google Play Integrity, prevent unauthorised use and enforce review limits. Serenyo's gateway may retain: Installation identifiers or public-key fingerprints. Review request identifiers and processing status. Spending-control and reservation records. Limited report metadata when a user reports an AI response. Google Play Integrity is used to verify relevant app and device information. Google Cloud and other service providers may also process technical network information needed to deliver and secure their services. 8. Legal basis for processing Where the GDPR or similar legislation applies, the legal basis depends on the processing activity. We may rely on: Performance of a contract to provide Serenyo's core functionality. Explicit consent for optional AI processing of sensitive or health-related reflections, where required. Legitimate interests for necessary security, access control and prevention of misuse, subject to applicable legal safeguards. Legal obligations where applicable law requires processing. Users may decline optional AI processing without losing access to local reflection features. 9. No sale of personal data or advertising use Serenyo does not sell personal information or use reflection content for advertising or data brokerage. OpenAI provides AI processing services for the optional Insights feature. Under OpenAI's standard API data policy, API inputs and outputs are not used to train OpenAI models unless the API customer explicitly opts in to data sharing for that purpose. 10. Future accounts and cloud sync Serenyo does not currently require user accounts or provide cloud synchronisation of personal reflection history. If accounts, cloud backup, subscriptions or cross-device synchronisation are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the additional processing. 11. AI-powered Insights AI Insights is an optional feature that helps users explore meaningful connections between their reflections. When a user selects reflections and explicitly agrees to a review: Serenyo prepares the selected reflection information. The information is transmitted over HTTPS to Serenyo's Google Cloud gateway. The gateway sends the information to OpenAI for AI processing. The generated insights are returned to the app for review. The user can choose whether to accept and save the insights locally. The feature uses OpenAI's API with response storage disabled through the store:false setting. However, this does not guarantee zero retention. OpenAI may temporarily retain API inputs and outputs for abuse monitoring, safety and security purposes under its applicable policies. AI-generated observations may contain inaccuracies or interpretations that do not reflect the user's experience. They are intended for personal reflection, not medical advice or diagnosis. 12. Data retention Local reflections and insights: Remain on the user's device until deleted through the app or removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Gateway records: Serenyo retains limited installation, request, spending-control and report metadata for operating and securing the AI pilot. These records are not subject to an automatic fixed deletion period in the current pilot and will be reviewed for removal when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations. OpenAI: Under standard API settings, abuse-monitoring logs may include customer content and are generally retained for up to 30 days, with exceptions where longer retention is legally required or reasonably necessary for safety and security. Temporary processing or caching may also occur under OpenAI's applicable API policies. Deleting a local reflection does not necessarily remove information already processed or temporarily retained by external providers. 13. Your choices and rights You can: Continue using Serenyo without AI Insights. Choose which reflections to submit for an AI review. Decline AI processing. Delete reflections and locally saved insights using the app's available controls. Contact us about applicable rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of personal information. Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw consent for future processing. Withdrawal cannot automatically recall a request already submitted or immediately erase external provider logs. For privacy enquiries or rights requests, contact serenyo.app@gmail.com. We will assess and respond in accordance with applicable law. Because Serenyo does not use personal accounts, identifying particular gateway records may be limited by the information available. 14. Children Serenyo is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. 15. Security Serenyo uses HTTPS encryption when transmitting information for AI processing. The gateway uses access controls, Google Play Integrity verification and installation-specific cryptographic credentials. OpenAI API credentials are stored on the server side rather than in the Android application. No system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their devices using appropriate security settings. 16. International transfers Serenyo's gateway and operational database are hosted using Google Cloud infrastructure in Belgium. Selected reflections are transmitted to OpenAI for AI processing. OpenAI's processing and retention may involve infrastructure or service providers outside Ireland or the European Economic Area. Serenyo does not currently guarantee EU-only OpenAI processing. International transfers are subject to applicable data-protection requirements and safeguards. 17. Changes to this policy We may update this Privacy Policy as Serenyo develops. Material changes involving personal data, AI processing, cloud storage, accounts or subscriptions will be explained through the app or another appropriate communication channel where required. 18. Contact For privacy questions or requests: serenyo.app@gmail.com Document version: 1.1 — Optional AI Insights pilot, 2 November 2026.
