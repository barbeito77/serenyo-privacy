Serenyo Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Serenyo is operated by Ruben Barbeito, trading as Serenyo. For privacy questions, contact us at serenyo.app@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Serenyo handles information when you use the app, website, or related services. It applies to the current MVP version of Serenyo and will be updated if we introduce accounts, cloud sync, subscriptions, AI-powered insights, or other features that change how data is processed.

3. Our privacy principles

  • Local-first by default: emotional reflections should stay on the user’s device unless clearly explained otherwise.
  • Data minimisation: collect only what is necessary for the app to work and improve safely.
  • No surprise AI: AI features, if introduced, should be explained before use.
  • No medical profiling: Serenyo is for reflection and wellbeing support, not diagnosis or treatment.

4. Information you may enter into Serenyo

Depending on how you use Serenyo, you may enter information such as:

  • mood or emotion selections;
  • reflection notes or journal entries;
  • contributing factors, such as sleep, work, family, stress, or social situations;
  • dates, times, and app interaction history related to your reflections;
  • preferences or settings inside the app.

This information may feel personal or sensitive. You should only enter information you are comfortable storing in Serenyo.

5. MVP local storage

In the MVP version, Serenyo is intended to work without an account. Reflection data is intended to be stored locally on your device. This means we do not intend to receive, read, sell, or share your reflection text through Serenyo’s own servers in the MVP version.

If you delete the app, clear app data, lose your device, or change device, locally stored reflections may be permanently deleted and may not be recoverable.

6. Usage analytics

Serenyo does not currently collect usage analytics.

Your private reflection notes and journal entries are not collected for analytics. If analytics are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated and the app will explain the change clearly.

7. Device and technical information

Serenyo does not currently send device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, approximate region, or diagnostic events to Serenyo-operated servers or third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

8. Legal basis for processing

If data protection law requires a legal basis, we may rely on:

  • performance of a contract, to provide the app and its core features;
  • legitimate interests, to maintain, secure, and improve Serenyo, where those interests are not overridden by your rights;
  • consent, where required for any future optional feature that processes personal data;
  • legal obligations, where we must comply with applicable law.

9. No sale of personal data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not intend to use your private reflection text for advertising or data brokerage.

10. Future accounts and cloud sync

If Serenyo introduces accounts, cloud backup, or cross-device sync, we may need to collect information such as your email address, account identifiers, authentication data, subscription status, and synced reflection data. We will update this Privacy Policy before or when those features are introduced.

11. Future AI-powered insights

If Serenyo introduces AI-powered summaries, pattern detection, reflection prompts, or enriched insights, we will explain what information is sent for AI processing, whether a third-party AI provider is used, how long information is retained, whether data is used for training, and how you can enable or disable the feature.

AI features should be optional where appropriate and should not be enabled for private reflection content without a clear explanation and consent flow where required.

12. Data retention

For local-first MVP data, reflections may remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Technical logs and analytics, if collected, should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for product improvement, security, legal, or operational purposes.

13. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal data we process about you. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

For local-only MVP data, deleting entries inside the app or deleting the app may remove the data from your device. If Serenyo does not receive or store that data on its servers, we may not be able to access, export, or recover it for you.

14. Children

Serenyo is intended for adults and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If this changes, additional protections and consent requirements will be added.

15. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. However, no app, device, network, or storage method is completely secure. You should protect your device with appropriate security settings, such as a passcode or biometric lock.

16. International transfers

If Serenyo later uses cloud, analytics, payment, or AI providers located outside your country, your information may be processed in other jurisdictions. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are significant, especially changes involving accounts, cloud storage, AI processing, subscriptions, or sensitive reflection data, we will provide notice in the app, by email, or by another appropriate method. We may ask you to review or accept updated terms before using new features.

18. Contact

If you have questions or requests about privacy, contact us at: serenyo.app@gmail.com.

Document version: 1.0 — MVP local-first version. Future AI/account features should trigger a version update.
